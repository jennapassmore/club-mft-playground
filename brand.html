Club MFT Brand Board

Messy-bun, trapper keeper energy.

The middle school energy comes from matrescence, the messy, identity-shifting transition into motherhood that's basically a second adolescence for moms. The logo is retro and the icons are hand-drawn, so we leaned in: a little imperfect, a little quirky, undone on purpose, like hair in a bun. It's a grown-up who's in touch with her inner child.

Why Seven colors, retro-leaning but rooted in the red and pink of Club MFT and MFT. Red is the hero. Petal and Lipstick carry the pink, Lilac and Sunshine add the warm retro, Midnight is the ink and Sand is the paper.

Midnight text works on Petal, Lilac, Lipstick, Sunshine and Sand. On Red and Midnight, use Sand. Never Midnight on Red.

IT TAKES A VILLAGE

Why The logo is retro, chunky and a little groovy, so it's always used as the supplied artwork and never retyped. It's recolored from the palette so it can sit on anything, with red as its home color.

Why The five icons are hand-drawn, one-color silhouettes, wobbly edges and all. That imperfection is the point, so we don't tidy them. Any palette color can fill them, and pairings are picked for contrast: dark icons on the pale colors, light ones on Red and Midnight.

Stickers

Why Trapper keeper energy: stickers instead of badges, because they're playful and nobody gets graded on them. The shapes come from classic sticker sheets (seals, hearts, ovals, rings), and the phrases carry the voice: affectionate, a little sweary, never preachy. The "matrescence" sticker is the whole idea in one word. Mixed sizes, tilts and curves keep them looking hand-placed and messy on purpose.
